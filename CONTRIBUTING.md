# Contributing

MCNexus is currently source-available for transparency, not an open contribution project.

Issues, bug reports, compatibility reports, documentation feedback, and security reports are welcome. Pull requests may be closed unless the change has been discussed and accepted in advance.

By submitting code, patches, text, designs, screenshots, ideas, or other materials, you confirm that:

- you have the right to submit the contribution;
- the contribution does not include secrets, private credentials, license keys, confidential files, or third-party material you are not allowed to share;
- you grant Magno Ciqueira permission to use, modify, publish, sublicense, and distribute the contribution as part of MCNexus and related products or services.

Do not submit private build materials, including:

- backend endpoints or secrets;
- signing certificates, provisioning files, or Store association files;
- private SDK binaries;
- full license keys or customer data.

For security issues, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of opening a public issue.
