Pricing

Free for your first 10 commercial activations.

Commercial projects distribute Beta, Demo and Trial editions with no limit and include their first 10 Full activations free, with a flat monthly fee starting only from the 11th Full activation. Zero commission on sales.

Community
Free & Open-Source

For free plugins and open-source projects. Unlimited activations, native installer on macOS and Windows, 1-click updates, and version rollback.

Commercial Start

Free up to 10 Full · then US$29/mo

US$290/year from the 11th Full activation (two months free). Unlimited Beta, Demo and Trial editions. MCNexus activates and installs on the workstation (Profile A).

  • Up to 500 live Full activations (Beta, Demo and Trial unlimited)
  • Unlimited products
  • MCNexus app + NexKeyRuntime C/C++ SDK (Profile A)
  • Full Commerce through Stripe* on the developer's own account (0% commission)
  • Automatic Demo/Trial to Full upgrade on the same key without reinstalling
  • Limited offline window
  • Release channel and critical notices
  • Signing key managed by the platform
  • Scheduled onboarding call to provision the tenant + email support (2 business days)

Commercial Pro

US$49 / month

US$490 per year, with two months free. Unlimited scale, studio air-gap and signing key ownership with MCNexus + SDK (Profile A).

  • Everything in Commercial Start
  • Unlimited Full activations (500+) and unlimited products
  • Standard offline window, configurable per license
  • Air-gapped activation and deactivation through exported files
  • Per-asset entitlements and protected download
  • Notices targeted by host, version and platform, with a scheduling window
  • The developer's own signing key (continuity), rotation and a 4-slot keyring
  • Scheduled onboarding call + email support (2 business days)
  • Customer portal · on the roadmap
  • Tenant-owned storage · on the roadmap

Commercial SDK

US$39 / month

US$390 per year, with two months free. The product embeds the SDK and activates its own license, with no MCNexus app in the loop (Profile B).

  • Unlimited activations and unlimited products
  • Full online and air-gap lifecycle via NexKeyRuntime C/C++ SDK
  • Full Commerce through Stripe* on the developer's own account
  • Detection of windowed versus headless processes, for a server or render farm
  • Release metadata and active notices queried via SDK API (UI and download by the integrator)
  • The developer's own signing key (continuity), recovery key and 4-slot keyring
  • Binaries for macOS universal and Windows x64, with nexkeyctl and binary license
  • The integrator distributes its own product, MCNexus is not in the loop
  • Scheduled onboarding call + email support (2 business days)

Custom integrations

Connection to existing infrastructure.

Existing systems can be connected directly to replace manual file distribution. Any active paid plan supports integrating existing infrastructure across the four platform connectors, or scoping custom engineering separately:

  • Licensing connector: native OpenKey or Cryptlex (already integrated in production); custom license engines* on the roadmap.
  • Checkout connector: native Stripe today; external checkouts (Lemon Squeezy*, Shopify*, Pix*) on the roadmap via standardized webhook adapters.
  • Release source connector: public or private GitHub Releases today; Cloudflare R2*, AWS S3* and GitLab* on the roadmap.
  • Custom host adapters & SLAs: adapters outside OFX, independent public manifests, and contractual SLAs are scoped and quoted case by case.
