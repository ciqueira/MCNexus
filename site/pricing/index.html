Pricing

Free for free software.

Paid from the moment the product is charged to the end user. Activation volume does not enter the calculation in any plan: no cap, no per-unit price.

Community

Free

For a product that is not charged to the end user. Automatic entry, no application and no approval.

  • Unlimited activations
  • Unlimited products
  • Per-machine node-lock and every license edition — beta, demo, trial and full
  • Limited offline window
  • Distribution through public GitHub Releases; MCNexus installs, updates and rolls back
  • License transfer between machines, self-service
  • Release channel and critical notices
  • The developer's own purchase URL, claim portal and support email
  • Community support
  • Signing key managed by the platform

Commercial A

US$75 / month

US$750 per year, with two months free. When the product is charged to the end user, from the first sale. MCNexus activates the license on the workstation.

  • Everything Community includes
  • Unlimited activations and products
  • Commerce through Stripe, on the developer's own account
  • Standard offline window, configurable per license
  • Air-gapped activation and deactivation through exported files
  • Per-asset entitlements and protected download
  • The developer's own signing key, rotation and a 4-slot keyring
  • Email support, best effort within 2 business days
  • Customer portal · on the roadmap
  • Tenant-owned storage, scheduled notices and white-label claim · on the roadmap

Commercial B

US$75 / month

Same price and same ceiling as Commercial A. The product embeds the SDK and activates its own license, with no MCNexus in the loop.

  • Unlimited activations and products
  • Full online lifecycle — activate, verify offline, synchronize, deactivate
  • Full air-gap lifecycle, the same as Commercial A
  • Detection of windowed versus headless processes, for a server or render farm
  • The same updates and notices channel
  • Recovery key and tenant keyring
  • Binaries for macOS universal and Windows x64, with nexkeyctl as the reference tool
  • The integrator distributes its own product — MCNexus is not in the loop
  • License for the compiled binaries · being defined

Outside a plan

What is quoted separately.

These do not fit inside a fixed monthly price and are not a plan tier. They are services, quoted case by case:

  • Integrating a new payment provider at a specific developer's request
  • An adapter for a new host, outside OFX
  • An independent public manifest
  • The developer's own licensing provider
  • A contractual SLA and portability guaranteed by contract

Contact

There is no signup and no self-service.

Each integration is reviewed and configured project by project. Self-service onboarding is on the roadmap. The first message should state the product, the supported platforms, and whether distribution is free or commercial.
